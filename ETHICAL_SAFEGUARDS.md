# Ethical Safeguards & Privacy Policy

## Web Scanning Ethics
Privacy Sentinel AI is designed to respect the rights of website owners and users.

1. **Robots.txt Compliance**: The scanner respects `robots.txt` directives. It identifies itself as `PrivacySentinelBot`.
2. **Rate Limiting**: Scans are rate-limited to prevent overloading target servers.
3. **Identification**: All requests include a `User-Agent` string identifying the bot and providing contact information.

## Data Handling
- We do not store scanned content permanently.
- We only extract privacy-related text.
- No personal data of website users is collected or stored.

## Usage Policy
This tool is for educational and privacy-auditing purposes. Do not use it to harass or scrape websites in violation of their terms of service.
